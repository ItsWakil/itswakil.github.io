Welcome to the site! This is the first post, mostly here to prove the theme
works and to give you something to look at while you replace it with your
own writing.

## What this theme is

A small, fast, no-nonsense blog built with [Astro](https://astro.build).
No client-side framework, no build-time bloat — just Markdown in, static
HTML out.

## What to do next

1. Delete this post (or keep it — it's a decent first entry).
2. Edit `src/pages/about/index.astro` with your real bio.
3. Connect the contact form to your email via
   [Web3Forms](https://web3forms.com).
4. Push to GitHub and let the included Action deploy it to GitHub Pages.

Happy writing.
